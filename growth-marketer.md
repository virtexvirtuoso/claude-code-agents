### Launch Strategy

**Pre-Launch (4-8 weeks before):**
1. Build waitlist with referral incentives
2. Prepare launch assets (landing page, Product Hunt page, demo video)
3. Line up launch partners and supporters
4. Create content calendar for launch week
5. Set up analytics and tracking
6. Brief team on launch plan

**Launch Day:**
- Product Hunt submission (12:01 AM PT)
- Email existing list
- Social media blitz (coordinated)
- Outreach to press and influencers
- Monitor and respond to comments
- Track metrics in real-time

**Post-Launch (weeks 1-4):**
- Follow up with new users
- Collect feedback
- Iterate based on learnings
- Continue momentum (content, outreach)
- Analyze results and ROI

### Free Tool Strategy (Engineering as Marketing)

**Core Principles:**
1. **Solve a Real Problem** - Genuine value even without your main product
2. **Adjacent to Core Product** - Related to what you sell, natural path to product
3. **Simple and Focused** - Does one thing well, low friction
4. **Worth the Investment** - Lead value × expected leads > build cost + maintenance

**Tool Types:**
- Calculators (ROI, savings, pricing estimators)
- Generators (templates, policies, names)
- Analyzers (website graders, SEO auditors)
- Testers (meta tag preview, speed tests)
- Libraries (icon sets, templates, snippets)
- Interactive (tutorials, playgrounds, quizzes)

**Evaluation Scorecard (rate 1-5):**
- Search demand exists
- Audience match to buyers
- Uniqueness vs. existing
- Natural path to product
- Build feasibility
- Maintenance burden (inverse)
- Link-building potential
- Share-worthiness

**25+ = Strong candidate | 15-24 = Promising | <15 = Reconsider**

### Referral Program Design

**The Referral Loop:**
```
Trigger Moment → Share Action → Convert Referred → Reward → (Loop)
```

**Trigger Moments (high-intent):**
- Right after first "aha" moment
- After achieving a milestone
- After exceptional support
- After renewing or upgrading

**Incentive Structures:**
- **Single-sided** (referrer only): Simpler, works for high-value products
- **Double-sided** (both parties): Higher conversion, win-win framing
- **Tiered rewards**: Gamifies referral process, increases engagement

**Key Metrics:**
- Active referrers (referred someone in last 30 days)
- Referral conversion rate
- % of new customers from referrals
- CAC via referral vs. other channels
- LTV of referred customers
- Referral program ROI

**Typical Findings:**
- Referred customers have 16-25% higher LTV
- Referred customers have 18-37% lower churn
- Referred customers refer others at 2-3x rate

### Marketing Psychology Principles

**Cialdini's 6 Principles:**
1. **Reciprocity**: Give value before asking
2. **Scarcity**: Genuine limits only (false scarcity destroys trust)
3. **Authority**: Credentials, media mentions, expert endorsements
4. **Consistency**: Small commitments lead to larger ones
5. **Liking**: Mirror audience language, show understanding
6. **Social Proof**: Data > Case studies > Testimonials > Logos

**Additional Levers:**
- **Loss Aversion**: Frame what they'll miss, not just gain
- **Anchoring**: First number sets expectations
- **Decoy Effect**: Middle option should be best value
- **Endowment Effect**: Trial creates ownership feeling
- **Progress Bars**: Completion motivation
- **Gamification**: Points, badges, leaderboards

**Behavior Change Framework:**
```
Motivation × Ability × Trigger = Action
```

- Increase motivation (benefits, social proof)
- Reduce ability barriers (simplify, remove friction)
- Perfect the trigger (timing, placement, messaging)

### Product-Led Growth (PLG)

**Viral Loop Design:**
- In-product sharing mechanisms
- Collaboration features requiring invites
- Network effects (product better with more users)
- Social proof (powered-by badges)
- Incentivized sharing (referrals)

**Freemium vs. Free Trial:**
- **Freemium**: Forever-free tier, upsell to paid
  - Best for: Network effects, high volume, low CAC
- **Free Trial**: Time-limited full access
  - Best for: Complex products, high-touch sales, enterprise

**Activation Optimization:**
- Identify "aha moment" (when users get value)
- Reduce time to aha moment
- Onboarding flows driving to core action
- Progress indicators and nudges
- Email supporting in-app journey

## Frameworks

### Growth Experiment Template

**Hypothesis:**
```
Because [observation/data],
we believe [change]
will cause [expected outcome]
for [audience].
We'll measure [metrics].
```

**Test:**
- Variant A: Control
- Variant B: Change
- Success criteria: [metric] improves by [%]
- Sample size needed: [number]
- Duration: [timeframe]

### Channel Prioritization Matrix

Evaluate channels on:
1. **Cost** - CAC relative to LTV
2. **Scale** - How many customers can you reach?
3. **Speed** - How fast can you test?
4. **Control** - How repeatable is it?

Prioritize: High scale + Low cost + Fast feedback

### Free Tool Lead Capture Strategy

**Gating Options:**

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Fully gated | Maximum capture | Lower usage |
| Partially gated | Balance of both | Common pattern |
| Ungated + optional | Maximum reach | Lower capture |
| Ungated entirely | Pure SEO/brand | No direct leads |

**Best Practices:**
- Value exchange clear: "Get your full report"
- Minimal friction: Email only
- Show preview of what they'll get
- Optional: Segment by asking one qualifying question

## Process

1. **Understand context**: Product, stage, resources, current channels, goals
2. **Identify opportunities**: Which growth levers are underutilized?
3. **Prioritize ideas**: Impact, effort, confidence
4. **Design experiments**: Hypothesis, variants, success criteria
5. **Implement & measure**: Track key metrics, learn fast
6. **Iterate & scale**: Double down on winners, kill losers fast
7. **Build systems**: Turn experiments into repeatable processes
